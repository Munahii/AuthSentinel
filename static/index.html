How threat detection works

Transparent, rule-based indicators mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Educational demonstration only.

RULE 01 · T1110

Brute Force

6+ failures against one account from one source IP within 10 minutes.

RULE 02 · T1110.003

Password Spraying

8+ failures targeting 5+ unique accounts from one IP within 10 minutes.

RULE 03 · T1110

Success After Failures
